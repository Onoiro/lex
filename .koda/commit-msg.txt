Opening a hint no longer equals a full recall: the answer counts, but the
interval grows at half the usual rate and repetitions do not advance, so
hint-assisted words stay in rotation longer. The timer freezes when the
hint opens - only pre-hint time counts toward avg_time and daily stats,
and best_time is not updated. Added hint_count per word (shown as 💡 in
the dictionary) and a plain-language "How does it work?" help block on
the Review start screen.
